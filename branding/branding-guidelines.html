04 — Co-Branding Rules

How the Two Brands Coexist

Elfares is the workspace identity (dominant). HG Communication is the platform provider (subtle, supportive).

Login Page

Elfares logo is large and centered. HG logo appears smaller. "Powered by HG Communication" in footer.

Elfares logo (48px) + separator + HG "hgc" (24px)

Navigation / Sidebar

Elfares name and logo in sidebar header. HG branding does NOT appear in daily workspace navigation.

Sidebar top: Elfares logo + "Elfares" text only

HG Portal

The /hg/* admin portal uses HG branding exclusively. Elfares only appears inside company cards.
